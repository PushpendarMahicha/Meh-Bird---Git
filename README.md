# MEH BIRD

**Dodge. Survive. Score.**

MEH BIRD is a 2D endless arcade game developed with Unity. Test your reflexes, avoid dangerous obstacles, survive unexpected encounters, and compete for the highest score.

## 🎮 About the Game

MEH BIRD takes the familiar endless-runner concept and adds its own collection of hazards and challenges. From moving vehicles to electrical dangers and laser attacks, every run is a test of timing and survival.

## ✨ Features

- **Endless Arcade Gameplay** – Keep playing and try to beat your personal best.
- **Varied Obstacles** – Encounter moving vehicles and other dangerous objects.
- **Laser Hazards** – Special coin interactions can trigger laser attacks.
- **Score System** – Earn points by navigating obstacles and collecting scoring items.
- **Online Leaderboard** – Compete with other players for the highest score.
- **Mobile Support** – Designed for Android devices, including ARM32 and ARM64.
